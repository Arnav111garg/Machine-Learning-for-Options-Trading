# Machine Learning for Options Trading

A quantitative research project exploring the application of machine learning, statistical modelling, and computational methods to options markets, volatility forecasting, derivatives pricing, and systematic strategy evaluation.

## Overview

**Machine Learning for Options Trading** is a structured research project focused on understanding how machine learning techniques can be applied to different aspects of options markets.

The project explores the intersection of financial mathematics, options analytics, statistical learning, and computational finance to investigate market behaviour, identify patterns, develop predictive models, and evaluate systematic trading strategies.

It covers the research lifecycle, starting with options data engineering and feature development, progressing through volatility forecasting, regime detection, option pricing, and market intelligence, and concluding with deep learning architectures and strategy backtesting.

The broader objective is to understand how data-driven models can complement traditional quantitative methods in analysing complex derivatives markets.

## Research Areas

### 1. Options Data Engineering

Exploring the collection, processing, cleaning, and organization of options market data. Focuses on developing reliable data foundations for quantitative analysis and machine learning applications.

### 2. Feature Engineering for Options

Investigating the construction of meaningful predictive features from options and underlying asset data. Includes exploring relationships between market variables, volatility measures, pricing characteristics, and derivatives-specific information.

### 3. Realized Volatility Prediction

Studying statistical and machine learning approaches to forecasting realized volatility. Examines the relationship between historical market behaviour, volatility indicators, and future volatility dynamics.

### 4. Volatility Regime Detection

Exploring methods for identifying changing volatility environments and market regimes. Focuses on understanding how different market conditions influence the behaviour of options and volatility-related models.

### 5. Machine Learning Option Pricing

Investigating data-driven approaches to options valuation and the application of machine learning as a complement to traditional pricing frameworks.

### 6. Mispricing Detection Systems

Exploring quantitative methods for identifying deviations between observed option prices and model-based valuations. Focuses on pricing discrepancies, predictive signals, and statistical analysis.

### 7. Surface Forecasting & Dynamics

Studying the behaviour and evolution of implied volatility surfaces across different strikes and maturities. Investigates approaches for modelling and forecasting changes in volatility surface characteristics.

### 8. Options Flow Intelligence

Exploring options market activity, trading flow, and derivatives-related signals to understand how market participation and trading behaviour relate to price and volatility dynamics.

### 9. Dealer Positioning Models

Investigating dealer positioning and its relationship with options market structure, liquidity, hedging activity, and underlying asset behaviour.

### 10. Options Sentiment Machine Learning

Exploring machine learning techniques for analysing sentiment-related information and investigating its potential relationship with options market behaviour.

### 11. Deep Learning Architectures

Studying advanced neural network architectures and their potential applications in options analytics, volatility forecasting, financial time-series modelling, and predictive research.

### 12. ML Strategy Backtesting

Exploring the evaluation of machine learning-driven strategies through historical data and systematic experimentation. Focuses on performance assessment, model validation, risk analysis, and the limitations of backtesting.

## Research Methodology

The project follows a structured quantitative research workflow:

1. **Data Engineering:** Collect, clean, transform, and organize options market data.
2. **Feature Development:** Construct relevant statistical, financial, and market-based features.
3. **Exploratory Analysis:** Investigate relationships, distributions, patterns, and market characteristics.
4. **Modelling:** Apply statistical learning and machine learning methodologies.
5. **Prediction & Evaluation:** Examine predictive performance using appropriate evaluation metrics and validation techniques.
6. **Strategy Research:** Investigate how model outputs can be evaluated within systematic options strategies.
7. **Backtesting:** Assess historical behaviour, performance, and robustness under realistic assumptions.

## Key Research Themes

The repository brings together several important areas of quantitative finance:

- **Options Analytics:** Derivatives data, pricing characteristics, and implied volatility.
- **Volatility Modelling:** Realized volatility forecasting, regime detection, and volatility surfaces.
- **Machine Learning:** Feature engineering, predictive modelling, and deep learning.
- **Market Intelligence:** Options flow, dealer positioning, and sentiment analysis.
- **Quantitative Strategy Research:** Model-driven signals, strategy evaluation, and historical backtesting.

## Research & Evaluation Principles

A key emphasis of this project is understanding not only how machine learning models perform, but also whether their results are statistically meaningful and economically relevant.

Important considerations include:

- Avoiding look-ahead bias and data leakage.
- Using appropriate time-series validation techniques.
- Comparing machine learning models against meaningful baselines.
- Accounting for transaction costs, bid-ask spreads, and slippage in strategy evaluation.
- Examining model robustness across different market conditions.
- Understanding model assumptions, limitations, and potential overfitting.

Importantly, predictive accuracy, pricing accuracy, and trading profitability are different objectives and should be evaluated separately.
